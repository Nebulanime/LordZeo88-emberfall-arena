The arena is burning. The ember chose you.
Survive the waves, gather shards, level up — and break the Warden every 5 waves.

HOW TO PLAY

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Mouse — aim  ·  Hold click / Space — shoot
  • Auto-fire is ON by default (F to toggle) — it auto-aims at the nearest enemy
  • Kill enemies → collect ◆ shards → level up, pick 1 of 3 upgrades
  • Boss every 5 waves. Watch the red bar.

CONTROLS

  • P / Esc — pause
  • M — mute  ·  1 / 2 / 3 — pick upgrade
  • R — restart after game over
  • Combo: kills within 3s raise your score multiplier.
